Hardest #1

Intangible
Services

Consulting, professional services, SaaS — the value is real, but you cannot hold it, photograph it, or weigh it. Buyers need to feel certainty before they write the cheque.

If you cannot show the before and after in under 10 seconds, you are asking buyers to take a leap of faith.

02
Hardest #2

Death by
Committee

The average B2B buying group has 6–10 stakeholders, each with different objections, priorities, and definitions of value. Marketing to one person means losing the other nine.

Your campaign needs a message that works in the boardroom and the IT department at the same time.

03
Hardest #3

The
Complexity Trap

Your product does remarkable things. The engineers love explaining it. But buyers do not buy specs — they buy outcomes. Drowning them in features kills deals before they start.

Simplicity is not dumbing it down. It is finding the one sentence that makes someone say, "I need that."

4 / 7
04
Hardest #4

The
Long Game

When a sale takes 12–18 months, attribution breaks down, budgets reset, and contacts change jobs. The campaign that planted the seed is long forgotten by the time the deal closes.

Long-cycle marketing is not about generating leads. It is about staying present in the buyer's mind for a year without being annoying.
OwlSurf
5 / 7
05
Hardest #5

The
Commodity Cage

When every competitor looks identical — same features, same case studies, same price — marketing becomes noise. Buyers default to who they already know, or whoever is cheapest.

Differentiation is not a positioning statement. It is a genuine reason someone tells their colleague, "Use these guys."
