# Design - Presentation Palette Perfect

A locked Hallmark design system for the OwlSurf credentials deck. This is a portfolio PDF replacement for Indian and international B2B buyers, with emphasis on chemical, industrial, and technical markets.

## Genre
Editorial with technical restraint.

## Macrostructure Family
- Marketing deck pages: Split Studio. Each slide pairs one claim with one proof surface.
- Proof pages: Stat-led proof strip plus visual case evidence.
- Content pages: Long-document discipline compressed into slide-sized fragments.

## Theme
- Paper: dark industrial ink, not pure black.
- Accent: OwlSurf teal, used as signal and focus, not flood.
- Panels: translucent technical ledgers with hairline rules.
- Imagery: real client creatives, badges, logos, and product proof. No fake browser chrome.

## Typography
- Display: Montserrat, weight 800-900, uppercase for credentials rhythm.
- Body: Palanquin, weight 400-600, plainspoken and readable.
- Outlier: Lora italic for one emphasis phrase at a time.
- Tracking: display uses normal tracking, labels use measured uppercase tracking.

## Spacing
4-point scale via `tokens.css`. Slide layouts should use stable grids and avoid nested cards.

## Motion
- Reveal pattern: one entrance per slide, transform and opacity only.
- Microinteractions: quiet hover states, no celebratory effects.
- Reduced motion: opacity-only fallbacks where spatial motion is avoidable.

## CTA Voice
- Primary: direct portfolio action, e.g. `Review case studies`.
- Secondary: contact action, e.g. `Start a conversation`.
- Buttons should stay single-line on mobile.

## What Pages Must Share
- OwlSurf dark paper, teal accent, and restrained proof-led tone.
- Claims must be paired with evidence, examples, sectors, client logos, case visuals, or platform partner badges.
- Copy should be concise enough to replace a PDF credentials deck.

## What Pages May Differ On
- Case-study brand colors.
- Whether proof is a logo wall, creative carousel, service ledger, or contact close.
- Density, as long as each slide remains readable in three seconds.

## Exports
See `tokens.css` for the active portable token set.
